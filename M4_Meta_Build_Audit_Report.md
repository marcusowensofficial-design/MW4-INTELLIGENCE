## 📌 Executive Summary
In competitive 6v6 Multiplayer on Modern Warfare 4 launch maps, gunfights are primarily decided by **Aim Down Sight (ADS) speed**, **Sprint-to-Fire (STF) recovery**, and **predictable recoil patterns**.

The **M4 (Assault Rifle)** serves as the undisputed all-rounder workhorse of MW4, firing at 810 RPM with a 5-shot kill ceiling out to 28 meters. This 5-attachment setup is mathematically engineered to eliminate horizontal shake while retaining rapid aim snap and generous 45-round magazine sustain.
