Modern Warfare 4 Launch Patch Notes
October 21
October 21, 2026
by Infinity Ward
MW4
Patch Notes
What a week it's been!
First, a huge thank you to everyone who has jumped into Campaign Early Access. The response has been incredible, and seeing the excitement, reactions, and conversations surrounding the Campaign has been something special for all of us at Infinity Ward. Our team has spent years bringing this experience to life, and finally getting to share it with you has made this week one to remember. For those who haven't played yet, jump in and experience it yourself.
And now, DMZ Early Access is underway! The excitement surrounding DMZ has been amazing to see, and we're looking forward to hearing about your first deployments into Hajin. From the discoveries you make to the stories you bring back from the Exclusion Zone, we'll be following along closely, reviewing your feedback, and continuing to make improvements as we head toward launch.
We also want to thank everyone who participated in the Multiplayer Beta. Your feedback helped us identify areas that needed attention, and the work hasn't stopped since the Beta ended. Over the past several weeks, we've been making adjustments, addressing feedback, and refining the experience across the game.
With launch just days away, we want to walk you through the changes, improvements, and fixes we've made as we prepare to welcome everyone into Modern Warfare 4 on October 23. Our pre-launch Patch Notes cover updates to Weapons, Movement, Spawns, and more, along with additional fixes and polish across the game. There’s a lot to get into, so let’s dive in.
MULTIPLAYER CONTENT
Modern Warfare 4 launches with a total of 20 Multiplayer maps across the full experience, including 12 Core 6v6 maps, three Ground War maps, four Gunfight maps, and Kill Block. We’ve also got over a dozen Multiplayer modes, 33 weapons, 33 Operators, and a full progression journey through Level 55 and beyond, all ready for you on day one.
PROGRESSION & PRESTIGE
MW4 is packed with content to earn throughout the journey, including two Prestige paths for the first time in Call of Duty. No matter which path you choose, you’ll unlock Operator Skins, Weapon Blueprints, and more inspired by Infinity Ward’s past games.
All 10 of our Prestige icons
pay homage to our history, and our
Prestige Master and Prestige Legend icons
honor those who go the extra mile. And if you’re a true OG, we’ve also got you covered throughout Prestige Master with the full selection of legacy Prestige icons from across the franchise, including all the classic Modern Warfare games.
Modern Warfare 4 offers a deep Weapon Camo and Attachment grind, and each Optic features multiple Reticles to unlock, including multiple color variations. There’s plenty to start earning progress toward on day one, whether you’re playing MP or DMZ... yes, even the elusive blue dot.
The Weapon Camo progression journey is the deepest we’ve ever built in a Modern Warfare game, with a huge collection to chase across Multiplayer, DMZ, and once Season 1 kicks off, Call of Duty: Warzone. MW4’s Weapon Camo grind is shared across all of these modes, and Special Camo Challenges in particular will automatically adjust to the specific mode you’re playing. Once you complete a Special Camo Challenge in any mode, that Special Camo will be available in all modes.

MODES
Over a dozen Multiplayer modes are available at launch, including returning favorites and several new additions (including Hardcore). Get ready to jump into these brand-new modes on day one, plus returning classics including TDM, Domination, Hardpoint, Kill Confirmed, Free-For-All, Frontline, Search & Destroy, Gunfight, and Gun Game.
New Modes
Inflation
Eliminate enemies and collect their cash. The more you carry, the bigger the target on your back. Players who reach the High Value Target threshold are revealed to both teams, creating a constant choice between hunting enemy HVTs and protecting your own.
Hijack
Fight for control of the Data Spike, then carry, throw, or pass it on the way to the enemy Comm Station. Plant it to begin scoring and defend the upload to earn the full bonus.
Collateral
A round-based tactical mode combining Search & Destroy mechanics with a Buy Phase. Start each match with a Pistol and $800, earn cash through objective play and eliminations, then spend it between rounds to build your loadout.
Combat Outpost
Ground War expands into a large-scale fight for territory. Capture Outposts to score and establish new spawn locations, then reinforce them to unlock increasingly powerful team rewards.
Infected and the new Possession mode are also dropping next week as a Halloween treat. For more intel on everything coming at launch, check out our Maps & Modes blog with all the details.
PROVING GROUNDS: MOBILITY COURSE
Building on the Mobility Course available during the Beta, Proving Grounds brings together movement practice and a variety of combat training exercises in one place. Whether you’re looking to improve your movement, sharpen your combat skills, or get more familiar with gameplay mechanics, Proving Grounds gives you the space to practice before jumping into a match or test your skills for a high score.

WEAPON TUNING
We received a lot of feedback about TTK and the pace of our game during the first weekend of Beta. We had a short window to make adjustments and collect data, so we made the choice to swing hard on changes in Weekend 2. Gunfights in the second weekend of Beta felt more meaningful; players had a little more breathing room and the pace of our game felt better across all game modes. We collected a lot of feedback and in-game data, and the TTK from Weekend 2 was strongly preferred by all skill levels.
While there was a clear player preference for Weekend 2’s TTK, we did not get the balance between weapons and classes exactly right. Since then, our team has spent countless hours and late nights playtesting and iterating to improve balance between all our weapons.
SMGs are now more efficient in close to mid-range combat, powerful one hit kill weapons are tempered with flinch and damage tuning, and all weapons have had a deep balance pass to ensure the weapon you pick is a choice, not a requirement.
We have also combed through all our attachments and tuned them to make sure Gunsmith allows flexibility and balance. Advanced Weapon Stats are also available at launch, so you’ll know everything you need to make the right choice for your playstyle.
Our weapon pillars are:
Distinction – Classes and weapons have clear, unique roles on the battlefield.
Balance – Players can find success with every tool available.
Feel – Weapons feel awesome, visceral, and fun.
These pillars must work together in harmony to deliver the best version of Modern Warfare 4. Our team is excited about the tuning we have arrived at for launch. We will be playing right alongside you and updating our game to hone it to perfection.
WEAPON PROGRESSION
The MW4 weapon system provides deep weapon customization with a variety of attachments and modifications. Our weapon progression is a streamlined approach to grow your arsenal without friction, respecting your time and reducing the grind to acquire functional customization.
No redundant unlocks. The speed of weapon progression increases with every shared attachment you’ve earned.
Once unlocked, shared attachments are immediately available for every weapon that can use them. Every attachment is a valuable piece of equipment that makes your entire arsenal more powerful.
Since the Beta we have worked to improve how weapon progression displays weapon unlocks instead of weapons levels. We’ve also improved the visualization of weapon progress after a match to more clearly represent a how progress is skipped with shared attachments.
WEAPON PRESTIGE
For those of you who want an even deeper progression journey, we’ve got you covered. Weapon Prestige is back for players who want a more dedicated grind to their favorite weapon. If a player chooses to Prestige their weapon, all attachment on that weapon (including shared attachments) become locked, and the weapon moves to a classic level system unlock. The shared attachments will remain unlocked on other weapons that have not been Prestiged.
OPTIC & RETICLE CUSTOMIZATION
We have added a new Optic Rail Placement feature in Gunsmith that allows you to tailor your weapon’s ADS sight picture exactly to your preference.
This is a completely cosmetic adjustment that can have a profound effect on the feel of your weapon. We also allow tuning from the ADS perspective in Gunsmith, so there is no guesswork involved in dialing placement down to perfection. What you see in Gunsmith is what you get in game.
Reticle Customization is also available on optics, with a small but significant improvement made to thermal optics. When you earn and equip custom reticle for a thermal, it comes with a completely different thermal effect. If you find a thermal optic you like but don’t enjoy that specific thermal effect, we have created a variety of authentic and distinct thermal options that are available when you customize our thermal optics reticle.

PERK TUNING
We’ve buffed some Perks based on Beta feedback:
Battle Hardened will now more effectively negate most of the effects from Tactical grenades like the Flash, Concussion, and Screamer Mine.
Resupply has been buffed to provide your first recharge faster.
Blood Rush has been updated so that pressing the sprint key automatically triggers Tactical Sprint by default. If you prefer to manage this directly, head towards the Movement Advanced Settings to adjust it.
PLAYER MOVEMENT
Movement in Modern Warfare 4 is about playing the way you want, whether you prefer being a tactical elite operator carefully clearing rooms, an action hero star mantling over cars and sliding under obstacles, or a heavy gunner holding down an objective. Since the Beta, we have focused hard on making sure transitions between actions are balanced, feel grounded, and allow a clear benefit at a clear cost. The results are smoother movement that feels more authentic than ever before.
Another area we have focused on improving is how enemy players move. We found several bugs where the third-person and first-person experience did not match, misrepresenting the enemy’s actions, creating some unrealistic looking movements, and allowing a few snaking opportunities. All the issues we identified have been fixed, and we will carefully monitor for any additional changes.
Lastly, we have kept the change from Beta to make Tactical Sprint not available by default. It lives on the Perk Blood Rush, which is now in the second tier. Since the player is using a valuable Perk slot to Tactical Sprint, we removed the additional sprint out penalty that Tac Sprint typically incurs, and for the benefit of thumbs and controllers worldwide, the default input for Tactical Sprint is now “single tap run.” This means when you activate sprint with Blood Rush equipped, you will Tac Sprint as long as stamina allows. For players who want full autonomy over when they use their Tac Sprint, we still have “double tap” and “single tap sprint” options available.
We’re excited for you to get your hands on our updated movement experience on the new maps we have available for launch.
MULTIPLAYER SPAWNS
Spawn work has continued since the Beta, including further iteration on the changes we made to player separation, squad spawning, and Hardpoint spawn predictability. Spawns have been reoriented around consistent flow and making sure you’re never far from the fight.
KILLSTREAKS
MW4 features 17 Killstreaks at launch, any of which can be attached to individual Multiplayer loadouts for the first time in a Modern Warfare game.
We are launching with our new Smart Streak Later where performing objective tasks like capturing a point in Domination will contribute towards your Killstreak meter. Killstreak tuning in the second weekend of the Beta that increased the cost of all streaks will remain for launch, meaning UAVs are 5 kills (or 4 kills with the Hardline perk equipped).
While the team is happy where most of these landed in the Beta, two specific changes have been made for launch:
UAV health has been increased from 100 to 150, following feedback that UAVs were too easy to shoot down after their health was reduced during the Beta.
Wheelson pathing and targeting logic have been reworked to be more aggressive and prevent confusion when targets change quickly.

EVENTS & REWARDS
We’re taking a new approach to Events in Modern Warfare 4. From launch throughout the live seasons, we’re focused on providing a variety of events including some all-new formats and returning favorites. Event rewards include Apex Attachments, Weapon Camos, Calling Cards, Operator Skins, and more.
As we focus on delivering more compelling events, Weekly Challenges will not be available in Season 1. We want each Event to feel worth your time and effort unlocking new playable content and rewards.
We’re also introducing reoccurring Events so that anyone who misses the chance to complete a full track can get another chance to earn that top reward. Additionally, you can earn camo rewards for completing multiple events. Our first Event kicks off right on launch day, and we’ll be keeping an eye on the feedback here for future seasons.
