			Patch Notes
        











October 28, 2026
 
Today's update brings more fixes and improvements across Multiplayer and DMZ.
 
In Multiplayer, we have our first weapon tuning update. We’ve also addressed issues with Challenges, Killstreaks, the Quick Fix Perk, and general gameplay.
 
For DMZ, we've closed exploits and resolved some issues with Loadouts and Exfil.
 
We're continuing to monitor your reports, prioritize fixes, and roll out improvements across Modern Warfare 4. Keep the feedback coming, and we'll keep the updates rolling.
 
PLAYLIST

Updated Oct 27
Shipment 24/7 (New) 
Frontline 
Inflation 
Collateral 
Kill Block: 6v6 Gunfight (New)  
Gun Game (New) 



 

Playlist Update: Free For All
Score limit remains at 30
Maximum 2 player party limit

 
A note on FFA: Yesterday we noticed that FFA matches were often going to time. We made a quick tweak to the score limit to help with this, and we've seen the feedback on that. We've adjusted the score limit back to 30 for our FFA mains, and in a later backend update we will restore Final Killcam, as it has been in recent MW titles.
 
GLOBAL
 
MOVEMENT
 
We are aware of a movement exploit where the player can immediately dive after a sprint jump to gain a boost of speed. To mitigate this short-term, we have reduced the dive's velocity by 23%. We are working on a long-term solution to fully fix this exploit in a way that the dive's velocity would not be impacted. We'll share more in a future update.
 
UI/UX

Fixed an issue where progress counters were missing from Camo and Special Camo unlock requirements across several weapon categories.
Fixed an issue where the Fungal Grid Camo was not visible in the Camo selection menu.
Fixed an issue where, in match, the Tracked & Near Complete Camo menu would not automatically track the next Camo Challenge after unlocking a Camo.
Fixed showing military Camos as locked after the player has Prestiged the weapon.
Fixed an issue where Prestiging from the DMZ FOB would not correctly reset Multiplayer Loadouts.
Fixed an issue where Calling Card unlock requirements could overlap with the progress bar.
Fixed an issue where the Crossfire and Argonaut Killstreaks displayed incorrect icons in the Multiplayer Killfeed.
Fixed an issue on PC where players could not click and drag the scrollbar in the Tracked & Near Complete Calling Cards menu. 
Fixed an issue where removing an attachment from a Blueprint and then re-adding it would not restore its unique visual while still in the menu.

 
MULTIPLAYER
 
Our first broad weapon tuning pass is focused on normalizing hit location damage for a more consistent TTK and general improvements to SMG performance.

BUG FIXES

Fixed a visual issue where the Mosin-Nagant's firing pin could appear in the incorrect position after firing the final round.
Fixed an issue where taking damage would not interrupt health regeneration from the Quick Fix Perk.

 
We noticed an issue where Quick Fix would continue to regenerate health even after taking damage. This was causing players to be more tanky than intended. Damage will now properly interrupt Quick Fix.
 

Addressed an exploit involving the Riot Shield entering out of bounds areas.
Improved the visibility of UAVs on Sentry.
Fixed an issue where certain Melee Kills, Backstabs, and Takedowns were not tracking correctly for some Challenges.
Increased the maximum range cutoff for Point Blank Challenge tracking.
Decreased the minimum range cutoff for Longshot Challenge tracking.
Fixed an issue where players would incorrectly appear as Reece when loading into Gun Game, regardless of their selected Operator.
Updated the Quick Fix Perk hitmarker icon.

 
DMZ
 
The intention for Possum is to give a high level operator a second chance. With the current iteration, Possum was being used as a combat mechanic instead of a survival mechanic. These tweaks are meant to prevent players from getting right back into the fight which they had just lost. We will be watching the use of Possum with this change to see if it has the desired effect.

We’ve adjusted the Possum Trait so that you are now unable to activate Possum while the team that killed you is nearby or until 4 minutes have passed. This range and duration is tunable going forward.

 
BUG FIXES

Addressed a bug where Possum could revive players who had already abandoned their Operator, which would put them in an unplayable state. 
Fixed an issue where players could encounter a black screen after their entire squad was eliminated while a teammate had the Possum Perk equipped.
Fixed an issue where using the 3D Printer to add items to the Backpack during deployment could cause players to deploy into DMZ without their Free Loadout weapons or equipment.
Closed several exploits in DMZ.
Fixed an issue where weapons could unintentionally drop when moving them from the Primary Weapon slot to the Backpack.
Fixed an issue where players were not receiving the 3-Plate Armor Vest reward upon reaching DMZ Level 37.
Fixed an issue where weapons could disappear after repeatedly swapping between weapon slots while using a Backpack with weapon storage.
Fixed an issue where the Airbase Access Card could not be upgraded during a Story Mission if it was stored in the Secure Slot.
Fixed an issue where automatically marked Order mission items could lose their Ping markers after players pinged a loot container.

 
KNOWN ISSUES

We're aware of an issue where the Patriot XMR Platinum Camo Challenge is not tracking correctly in the UI. The challenge can still be completed and progressed despite not displaying accurately. We are working on a fix.
We're investigating some Calling Card challenges not tracking as intended.
We're aware of an issue with Best Play selection logic in Multiplayer modes.
Players who enter Classic Prestige via DMZ menus instead of MP menus are unable to make new MP classes until previously created MP classes with relocked Loadout items are deleted.

 
Track live issues on our official Trello board and follow CODUpdates for the latest news.














October 25, 2026
 
We've been keeping a close eye on the issues you've been reporting and working through them as quickly as we can. Today's update addresses several of those issues, along with additional gameplay adjustments and improvements.
 
There's more work ahead, and we're not slowing down. The team is also looking at potential weapon tuning changes for a future update. We'll continue rolling out fixes, making adjustments, and keeping you informed along the way. 
 
GLOBAL

Fixed an issue on XBOX and PC where players using the full Korean language setting could not launch Black Ops 7 from MW4.

 
UI/UX

Fixed an issue where weapon stats were not displayed for locked Attachments in Gunsmith.
Updated the Prestige reset process to ensure Loadouts are properly cleared when Prestiging.
Fixed a visual issue in the Optic Rail Placement menu.
Fixed an issue where the Resource Management Trait description would scroll too fast to read.
Fixed an issue where the Patriot XMR, Oden, and Cronen Squall were incorrectly classified as Battle Rifles in various places throughout the game.
Fixed an issue where DMZ Lieutenant Intel unlock requirements and the eliminated indicator could disappear when hovering over previously eliminated Lieutenants on the Boss Board.

 
MULTIPLAYER
We’ve rolled back a previous fix for “bomb stretching” in Search and Destroy due to a knock-on issue in which players were unable to pick up the bomb in certain positions. We will be addressing bomb stretching again in an update as soon as we are able.
 
We’re also looking at XP earn rates in Multiplayer modes and will be making some changes, starting with this update.
 

XP for 1 kill has been boosted from 100 to 300 in Search and Destroy and Collateral.
Fixed an issue on Collateral where the bomb could disappear after players interacted with it.
Fixed an issue where the game could briefly freeze when editing a Loadout for the first time during a match.
Fixed an issue where the announcer's game mode introduction would not play at the start of Frontline or Hijack matches.

 
DMZ

Fixed an issue where players could bypass the Hospital Elevator objective during the Critical Extraction Story Mission.
Decreased Drill Charge damage against enemy Colossus.
Fixed an issue where a Sedan could be missing from its marked location on the Tac-Map.
Fixed an issue where enemy AI could clip through the Crashed Drone.
Fixed an issue where the Last Stand icon appeared oversized on the Minimap.
Fixed an issue where purchasing saved Builds from modified Blueprints could incorrectly charge players the full cost of the original Blueprint.
Fixed an issue where players could attempt to deploy while items remained in their Overflow Stash. Note: Operators must reconcile their Overflow Stash before the next deployment.
Fixed several issues in the Breaking New Ground (onboarding) mission.

 
KNOWN ISSUES
Track live issues on our official Trello board and follow CODUpdates for the latest news.
 
RECENTLY UPDATED

Closed various DMZ exploits.
We've addressed an issue where players were being AFK kicked while spectating in some Multiplayer modes.

 














October 24, 2026
Another round of fixes and improvements is here for Modern Warfare 4, covering Multiplayer and DMZ.
 
Our teams continue to focus on improving stability, addressing bugs, resolving exploits, and making quality of life improvements across the game.
 
We're committed to delivering consistent updates and improving the experience for everyone. There's still plenty of work ahead, and we'll continue addressing issues and sharing our progress as we move forward.
 
Thank you for your continued feedback and support.
 
Happy hunting!
 
GLOBAL
 
Stability

Fixed several stability and crash issues for a better experience across all modes.

 
UI/UX

Fixed an issue where DMZ Stash options could incorrectly appear in the middle of the screen.
Fixed various instances where front end menus could close unexpectedly.
Fixed an issue on PC where the Split keybind would not update to reflect the player's custom Ping keybind, resulting in players thinking they were unable to split inventory stacks.
Fixed an issue where Finishing Move effects could remain playing after returning to the Create a Class menu.
Fixed an issue where DMZ Weapon Builds could incorrectly appear as usable in Core Multiplayer.
Fixed an issue where applying some weapon camos or reticles in DMZ Gunsmith could result in a deduction of DMZ cash.
Fixed an issue where editing a loadout could sometimes cause a non-Melee weapon to appear in the Melee slot of the in-game Loadout menu. 
Fixed an issue where a weapon’s attachments would not automatically reset when Prestiging in Gunsmith.

 
Weapons

Fixed an issue where an incorrect attachment could be equipped to the M4 and cause a visual bug on the weapon’s muzzle.
Fixed a visual issue where bullets could appear to float inside empty or partially used magazines for the La Sombra Venenosa Blueprint.

 
MULTIPLAYER

Improved spawn locations and respawn timing in Hijack.

 
Bug Fixes

Fixed an issue where the Portable Radar Field Upgrade would not display the Minimap while active in Hardcore matches.
Fixed an issue in Search and Destroy where players could defuse the bomb from an unintended distance or position.
Fixed an issue on Rooftops where the Deathstalker Killstreak could travel outside the map boundaries.
Fixed an issue where players' Favorite Operators were not properly equipped in Collateral.
Note: Weapon Prestiging and Player Prestiging are unavailable while actively searching for a match. We have updated the UI to clarify.

 
DMZ

Fixed even more areas where players could become stuck in rocks on Hajin.
Fixed an issue where Party Leaders could pull teammates out of DMZ without their consent, causing their Active Duty Operators to be marked as MIA.
Fixed an issue where objective markers could display on the wrong enemy during the Obtain Code steps of the Dark Zone Story Mission. 
Fixed an out-of-bounds issue in the FOB.
Fixed some instances of incorrect data displaying on looted Dog Tags and Bounties.
Fixed an issue where the Exfil helicopter could disappear while players were still inside.
Fixed an issue where AI reinforcements during the Shopping Mission were spawning too frequently.
Fixed an issue where the Philanthropist Order on Page 19 would not track Weekly Mission Challenges completed before the Order was unlocked.
Closed various exploits.

 
KNOWN ISSUES

The regional map animation while searching for lobbies has been temporarily disabled as part of a prospective fix for reports of hitching.
We're investigating an issue causing some players to be kicked while spectating in Multiplayer.
Players may experience a network error when in DMZ extracting with a looted Dog Tag on PC.

 
Track live issues on our official Trello board and follow CODUpdates for the latest news.
 
RECENTLY UPDATED

Addressed an issue causing some player models to appear in static or incorrect animations. This may result in enemy players appearing in unintended states.
Various stability and crash fixes for PC.















October 23, 2026

MODES 
 
Over a dozen Multiplayer modes are available at launch, including returning favorites and several new additions (including Hardcore). Get ready to jump into these brand-new modes on day one, plus returning classics including TDM, Domination, Hardpoint, Kill Confirmed, Free-For-All, Frontline, Search & Destroy, Gunfight, and Gun Game. 
 
New Modes 

Inflation 
Eliminate enemies and collect their cash. The more you carry, the bigger the target on your back. Players who reach the High Value Target threshold are revealed to both teams, creating a constant choice between hunting enemy HVTs and protecting your own.  


Hijack 
Fight for control of the Data Spike, then carry, throw, or pass it on the way to the enemy Comm Station. Plant it to begin scoring and defend the upload to earn the full bonus.  


Collateral 
A round-based tactical mode combining Search & Destroy mechanics with a Buy Phase. Start each match with a Pistol and $800, earn cash through objective play and eliminations, then spend it between rounds to build your loadout.  


Combat Outpost 
Ground War expands into a large-scale fight for territory. Capture Outposts to score and establish new spawn locations, then reinforce them to unlock increasingly powerful team rewards.

WEAPON TUNING 
 
We received a lot of feedback about TTK and the pace of our game during the first weekend of Beta. We had a short window to make adjustments and collect data, so we made the choice to swing hard on changes in Weekend 2. Gunfights in the second weekend of Beta felt more meaningful; players had a little more breathing room and the pace of our game felt better across all game modes. We collected a lot of feedback and in-game data, and the TTK from Weekend 2 was strongly preferred by all skill levels. 
 
While there was a clear player preference for Weekend 2’s TTK, we did not get the balance between weapons and classes exactly right. Since then, our team has spent countless hours and late nights playtesting and iterating to improve balance between all our weapons. 
 
SMGs are now more efficient in close to mid-range combat, powerful one hit kill weapons are tempered with flinch and damage tuning, and all weapons have had a deep balance pass to ensure the weapon you pick is a choice, not a requirement. 
 
We have also combed through all our attachments and tuned them to make sure Gunsmith allows flexibility and balance. Advanced Weapon Stats are also available at launch, so you’ll know everything you need to make the right choice for your playstyle. 
 
Our weapon pillars are: 

Distinction – Classes and weapons have clear, unique roles on the battlefield. 
Balance – Players can find success with every tool available. 
Feel – Weapons feel awesome, visceral, and fun. 

 
These pillars must work together in harmony to deliver the best version of Modern Warfare 4. Our team is excited about the tuning we have arrived at for launch. We will be playing right alongside you and updating our game to hone it to perfection. 
 
WEAPON PROGRESSION 
 
The MW4 weapon system provides deep weapon customization with a variety of attachments and modifications. Our weapon progression is a streamlined approach to grow your arsenal without friction, respecting your time and reducing the grind to acquire functional customization. 
 
No redundant unlocks. The speed of weapon progression increases with every shared attachment you’ve earned. 
 
Once unlocked, shared attachments are immediately available for every weapon that can use them. Every attachment is a valuable piece of equipment that makes your entire arsenal more powerful. 
 
Since the Beta we have worked to improve how weapon progression displays weapon unlocks instead of weapons levels. We’ve also improved the visualization of weapon progress after a match to more clearly represent a how progress is skipped with shared attachments. 
 
WEAPON PRESTIGE 
 
For those of you who want an even deeper progression journey, we’ve got you covered. Weapon Prestige is back for players who want a more dedicated grind to their favorite weapon. If a player chooses to Prestige their weapon, all attachment on that weapon (including shared attachments) become locked, and the weapon moves to a classic level system unlock. The shared attachments will remain unlocked on other weapons that have not been Prestiged. 
 
OPTIC & RETICLE CUSTOMIZATION 
 
We have added a new Optic Rail Placement feature in Gunsmith that allows you to tailor your weapon’s ADS sight picture exactly to your preference.  
 
This is a completely cosmetic adjustment that can have a profound effect on the feel of your weapon. We also allow tuning from the ADS perspective in Gunsmith, so there is no guesswork involved in dialing placement down to perfection. What you see in Gunsmith is what you get in game. 
 
Reticle Customization is also available on optics, with a small but significant improvement made to thermal optics. When you earn and equip custom reticle for a thermal, it comes with a completely different thermal effect. If you find a thermal optic you like but don’t enjoy that specific thermal effect, we have created a variety of authentic and distinct thermal options that are available when you customize our thermal optics reticle.

PERK TUNING 
 
We’ve buffed some Perks based on Beta feedback: 

Battle Hardened will now more effectively negate most of the effects from Tactical grenades like the Flash, Concussion, and Screamer Mine. 
Resupply has been buffed to provide your first recharge faster. 
Blood Rush has been updated so that pressing the sprint key automatically triggers Tactical Sprint by default. If you prefer to manage this directly, head towards the Movement Advanced Settings to adjust it. 

 
PLAYER MOVEMENT 
 
Movement in Modern Warfare 4 is about playing the way you want, whether you prefer being a tactical elite operator carefully clearing rooms, an action hero star mantling over cars and sliding under obstacles, or a heavy gunner holding down an objective. Since the Beta, we have focused hard on making sure transitions between actions are balanced, feel grounded, and allow a clear benefit at a clear cost. The results are smoother movement that feels more authentic than ever before. 
 
Another area we have focused on improving is how enemy players move. We found several bugs where the third-person and first-person experience did not match, misrepresenting the enemy’s actions, creating some unrealistic looking movements, and allowing a few snaking opportunities. All the issues we identified have been fixed, and we will carefully monitor for any additional changes. 
 
Lastly, we have kept the change from Beta to make Tactical Sprint not available by default. It lives on the Perk Blood Rush, which is now in the second tier. Since the player is using a valuable Perk slot to Tactical Sprint, we removed the additional sprint out penalty that Tac Sprint typically incurs, and for the benefit of thumbs and controllers worldwide, the default input for Tactical Sprint is now “single tap run.” This means when you activate sprint with Blood Rush equipped, you will Tac Sprint as long as stamina allows. For players who want full autonomy over when they use their Tac Sprint, we still have “double tap” and “single tap sprint” options available. 
 
We’re excited for you to get your hands on our updated movement experience on the new maps we have available for launch. 
 
MULTIPLAYER SPAWNS 
 
Spawn work has continued since the Beta, including further iteration on the changes we made to player separation, squad spawning, and Hardpoint spawn predictability. Spawns have been reoriented around consistent flow and making sure you’re never far from the fight. 
 
KILLSTREAKS 
 
MW4 features 17 Killstreaks at launch, any of which can be attached to individual Multiplayer loadouts for the first time in a Modern Warfare game. 
 
We are launching with our new Smart Streak Meter where performing objective tasks like capturing a point in Domination will contribute towards your Killstreak meter. Killstreak tuning in the second weekend of the Beta that increased the cost of all streaks will remain for launch, meaning UAVs are 5 kills (or 4 kills with the Hardline perk equipped). 
 
While the team is happy where most of these landed in the Beta, two specific changes have been made for launch: 

UAV health has been increased from 100 to 150, following feedback that UAVs were too easy to shoot down after their health was reduced during the Beta.  
Wheelson pathing and targeting logic have been reworked to be more aggressive and prevent confusion when targets change quickly.
