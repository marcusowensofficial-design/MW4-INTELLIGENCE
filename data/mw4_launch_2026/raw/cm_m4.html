M4 Frequently Asked Questions

Is the M4 meta in Modern Warfare 4?

The M4 is currently ranked as Absolute Meta in Modern Warfare 4. Its current pick rate is 22.8%, which shows how popular the weapon is compared to other options.

What is the best M4 Loadout?

The best M4 Loadout is the Versatile build. For the attachment you want to use: ZLR Type C Optic, XRK Mil-Tac Suppressor Muzzle, 18" LE Crosswind Barrel, Demo Dozer Stock Stock, SA Laz-500 Laser.

Is the M4 easy to use?

The M4 has a base EaseScore of 5.8/10. Easescore is a proprietary metric to evaluate how easy a gun is to use. The higher it is, the easier the gun is.

What is the M4 TTK or Time To Kill in Modern Warfare 4?

With the Versatile loadout, the M4 has an weighted average time to kill of 296ms up to 10m. Then 320ms up to 40m, 405ms up to 80m and 462ms up to 120m.

What are the best alternatives to the M4?

The best alternatives to the M4 are Han 86, Kastov 762, Hyeon Burst, Axion, Cronen Squall.

What is the M4 pick rate?

The current M4 pick rate is 22.8%. Pick rate is a useful indicator of how popular the weapon is in the current Modern Warfare 4 meta, but it should always be compared with its Loadout quality, TTK, recoil control, and overall ranking.

In which Call of Duty games is the M4 meta?

The M4 is currently part of the meta in Modern Warfare 4. Its exact ranking and best builds are listed for each game on this page.

How many attachments are available for the M4?

The M4 can be equipped with 69 different attachments. You can browse them all, with their unlock levels, in the Attachments section above.

How do you unlock camos for the M4?

Every camo for the M4 has its own challenge to complete, spread across 2 camo grinds: Weapon Camos, Weapon Prestige. You can track your progress for each of them with the CODMunity camo tracker.

Is the M4 getting more or less popular?

At the start of the tracked period the M4 had a pick rate of 58.38%; it now sits at 57.40%. The daily evolution is charted in the Pick Rate Evolution section above.
