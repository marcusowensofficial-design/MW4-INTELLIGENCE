## 1. Executive Summary & Loadout Architecture
- **Perk Slots**: **3 Discrete Slots** per loadout (Perk 1, Perk 2, Perk 3).
- **Equipped per Class**: **1 Perk per slot** (3 perks total per loadout).
- **Custom Loadout Capacity**: Up to **12 Custom Loadouts**.
- **Total Beta Perks Cataloged**: **18 Perks** (6 per slot).
- **Unlock Mechanics**: Gated strictly by **Player Account Rank**.
- **Beta Caps**: 
  - Weekend One (Aug 21–25): Capped at **Level 20**.
  - Weekend Two (Aug 28–Sep 1): Capped at **Level 30**.
  - High-tier preview perks (Level 31–48) are visible in Create-a-Class for full launch progression.

## 4. Advanced Game Mechanics & Tactical Rules

### 🎯 Total Removal of "Hipfire Bloom"
- **Mechanic**: Unlike previous titles where hipfire spread randomly expanded with RNG bloom bullets, MW4 has **zero hipfire bloom**.
- **Ballistic Implication**: Hipfire dispersion is a 100% fixed geometric cone dictated strictly by Gunsmith hipfire radius modifiers. Close-range hipfire laser builds on SMGs (ISO Nightshade, Rival-9) are mathematically reliable room clearers.

### 🌐 Networked Trip Wire Traps
- **Mechanic**: The new **Trip Wire** lethal item allows placing multiple anchors that link together into an explosive laser net across wide doorways and objective corridors.

### 🚀 Smoke Wall Field Upgrade
- **Mechanic**: Deploys a rocket-propelled linear smoke line creating an instantaneous full-lane visual barrier against enemy sniper sightlines.
